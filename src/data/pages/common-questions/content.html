A little clarity, before we begin

Common Questions

Planning a celebration brings plenty of questions. Here are a few answers to help you find your starting point—and the support that feels right for you.

We’re planning from overseas. Where do we begin?

Let’s start with a complimentary Zoom consultation. We’ll talk about your vision, preferences, budget, and practical needs, then break the planning into manageable steps.

Planning an event in another country can feel like a learning curve. I’m in your corner to help you navigate the choices, feel confident in your decisions, and enjoy the process.

Which planning service is right for us?

We’ll explore how much time you want to invest in planning, what you would enjoy handling yourself, and what you would feel comfortable delegating.

You may want full-service planning, help finding and booking your professionals, or coordination on the day itself. Together, we’ll choose the level of support that suits you and clarify who handles what.

How far in advance should we contact you?

Never too early to get in touch, especially if you have a particular date in mind. Connecting early gives us time to explore your options and reserve availability.

If your celebration is sooner, please still reach out. I’m experienced in working with shorter timelines and adapting when circumstances change. We’ll discuss what is possible within the time available.

Which areas do you serve?

I plan celebrations throughout Israel and have also brought my event-planning experience abroad.

Whether your family is gathering in Israel or you would like to bring a taste of Israel to your hometown, let’s talk about the location and occasion you have in mind.

How are your fees determined, and what do they include?

My fees reflect the scope of your event and the level of support you choose. We discuss your priorities and budget, then agree on the services and planning fee before we begin.

With full-service planning, you have an experienced, bilingual planner in your corner—helping navigate language differences, coordinate your professionals, and bring the practical details together with personal care.

Fees charged by venues and other service professionals are separate from my planning fee. We’ll clarify what is included so you can make informed decisions.

Can you help if we have already booked our venue or other professionals?

Absolutely. We can build on the arrangements you have already made and identify where you still need support.

I offer flexible assistance, including booking services and day-of-event coordination. You don’t need to start over—we’ll work together on what remains to be done.

Will you personally coordinate our event, or will an associate be involved?

When you book me as your day-of-event coordinator, I personally coordinate your celebration. For a large wedding, an assistant works alongside me.

If you prefer an option at a lower fee, you can book an associate event coordinator with my supervision. We’ll discuss the arrangements and make clear who will be working with you before you book.
