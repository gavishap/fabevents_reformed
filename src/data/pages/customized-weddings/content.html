The FAB approach—and what each service covers

  • Venue and professional sourcing: Included in full-service planning and the booking-agent service. Recommendations are matched to your parameters.
  • Planning and ongoing coordination: Full-service planning brings the arrangements together throughout the planning process.
  • Preferred pricing: Where available through our professional relationships, with costs discussed before you commit.
  • Booking-agent service: Introductions, advice, and bookings for a flat fee. You manage the event. On-site coordination is a separate service.
  • Event-day oversight: Included with full-service planning and day-of coordination. We review the agreed arrangements and coordinate the professionals on site.
